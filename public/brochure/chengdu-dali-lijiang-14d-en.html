The journey

Four distinct chapters, one coherent story

This route works especially well for a first or second journey to China. It balances headline experiences — pandas, ancient towns and dramatic mountain scenery — with places that reveal themselves more quietly: a Taoist trail, a Bai courtyard, a Naxi scholar's perspective and time to simply look across Erhai Lake.

Days 1–3

Qingcheng

Panda conservation, Dujiangyan's living water system and restorative time among bamboo-covered foothills.

Days 4–7

Dali

Erhai Lake, Cangshan Mountain and Bai culture, explored beyond the most photographed streets.

Days 8–10

Lijiang

Naxi heritage, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and a choice between gentle discovery and a deeper gorge walk.

Days 11–14

Chengdu

Tea houses, contemporary culture and Sichuan food — with a calm city base before the journey home.

Day by day

A considered 14-day rhythm

D1 · Sep 12

Arrive in Chengdu · Continue to Qingcheng Mountain

Your private driver meets you at the airport for the roughly 1.5-hour drive. If arrival time allows, visit the UNESCO-listed Dujiangyan irrigation system with your guide.

D2 · Sep 13

Panda volunteer programme · Qingcheng Mountain

Begin before 9am, when the pandas are most active, then walk Qingcheng's Taoist paths with a bilingual guide. Reference activity prices: CNY 1,200 and CNY 108 per person.

D3 · Sep 14

Six Senses red-panda experience · Hot springs

Join the resort's guided red-panda programme, then keep the afternoon for a private hot spring or spa. Reference programme price: CNY 2,566 per person, subject to current availability and rules.

D4 · Sep 15

Fly to Dali · First evening on Erhai Lake

A roughly 1.5-hour domestic flight and private transfer bring you to Yunnan. Keep the first evening open for the lake and the changing light.

D5 · Sep 16

Xizhou · Bai homes and indigo

Explore a living Bai community, meet craftspeople and try traditional tie-dyeing. Reference admission and programme price: CNY 150 per person.

D6 · Sep 17

Cangshan Mountain · Jizhao'an tea

Weather permitting, ride the main cableway into Cangshan's high landscape, then visit the mountainside nunnery for tea. Reference prices: CNY 320 return and CNY 20 per person.

D7 · Sep 18

Erhai's quieter shores · Drive to Lijiang

Spend about two hours with a private driving guide, stopping around Shuanglang, small viewpoints and fishing villages before continuing to Lijiang.

D8 · Sep 19

Baisha · Yuhu Village · Naxi stories

See Ming-era murals, visit a local embroidery school and trace Joseph Rock's years beneath Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. Reference private guided programme: CNY 800 per person.

D9 · Sep 20

Tiger Leaping Gorge guided hike

Take a roughly five-hour Middle Gorge route with a professional hiking guide. Difficulty, altitude and weather are assessed for your party. Reference price: CNY 1,550 per person.

D10 · Sep 21

Jade Dragon Snow Mountain · Blue Moon Valley

Ride the main cableway toward 4,506 metres, allow time to acclimatise, visit Blue Moon Valley and attend the Impression Lijiang performance. Reference prices: CNY 1,970 plus CNY 260 per person.

D11 · Sep 22

Fly to Chengdu · Taikoo Li at dusk

The roughly 1.5-hour flight returns you to Chengdu. Private airport transfer is approximately CNY 300 from Shuangliu or CNY 600 from Tianfu.

D12 · Sep 23

Chengdu Research Base · Creative city life

Enter at around 8am to see the pandas in their most active hours with a specialist guide, then explore Eastern Suburb Memory or Yulin Road. Reference panda visit: CNY 300 per person.

D13 · Sep 24

Kuanzhai Alley · Sichuan hotpot farewell

Spend the final afternoon with old Chengdu flavours, covered-bowl tea and a relaxed walk, followed by an authentic hotpot dinner adapted to your preferred heat level.

D14 · Sep 25

Private transfer · Return to Singapore

Your driver takes you to the airport. Your WR advisor remains reachable through the end of the journey, including last-minute changes and the trip home.
