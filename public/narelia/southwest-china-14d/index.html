07
Travel notes

What to know before departure

Mountain weather

Cangshan, Tiger Leaping Gorge and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain are weather-sensitive. Cable-car operations and hiking access may change at short notice.

Panda experiences

Keeper and volunteer activities are subject to animal-welfare rules and base operations. The optional Chengdu visit does not include hugging or holding pandas.

Private scenic drive

The Erhai programme is planned as a private chauffeur-driven scenic route, with quieter viewpoints and a light visit to Shuanglang.
